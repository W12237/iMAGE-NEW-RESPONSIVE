# Image Developments homepage direction

## Narrative

The homepage moves from dawn to evening: an intimate architectural opening, a sequence of three places revealed as chapters, a human-scale lifestyle pause, evidence of credibility, current stories, and a dark closing invitation. Forest acts as the structural color, cream as breathing space, and peach as a warm signal rather than decoration.

## 1. Opening / hero — “Space, imagined differently”

- **Purpose:** establish confidence and atmosphere immediately.
- **Composition:** full-viewport architectural image with a controlled dark veil; compact eyebrow and oversized title sit low in the frame; header is integrated into the image.
- **Scroll / motion:** brief branded curtain reveal, slow media scale, staggered title lines, and a minimal vertical scroll indicator.
- **Desktop:** title occupies roughly two-thirds of the width; secondary project note anchors the opposite lower corner.
- **Mobile:** tighter crop, four-line title, bottom-anchored copy, simplified scroll cue.
- **RTL:** title and metadata anchor from the right; line breaks are authored for Arabic; directional icons reverse.

## 2. Featured developments — “Three ways to belong”

- **Purpose:** make project discovery the homepage’s central experience.
- **Composition:** a forest introduction leads into a sticky full-viewport image stage. As project chapters progress, photography, counter, location, title, and short promise change together.
- **Scroll / motion:** desktop uses a sticky stage driven by ordinary vertical scroll; image crossfades/scales while text enters with restrained lateral movement. Mobile uses stacked project chapters to avoid trapped or overly long sticky scrolling.
- **Desktop:** image occupies most of the viewport; information sits in a strong vertical rail.
- **Mobile:** each project is a 78vh image chapter with content integrated at the bottom.
- **RTL:** information rail and counters change sides; movement direction follows reading direction.

## 3. Architectural/lifestyle pause — “Life between the lines”

- **Purpose:** shift from development names to the lived experience between buildings.
- **Composition:** edge-to-edge landscape image with one large sentence and a small caption.
- **Scroll / motion:** gentle parallax only; copy remains calm and readable.
- **Desktop:** panoramic crop and broad negative space.
- **Mobile:** portrait crop with protected text contrast.
- **RTL:** authored Arabic phrasing and right-anchored caption.

## 4. Credibility — “Measured in lives, not landmarks”

- **Purpose:** communicate scale without KPI cards.
- **Composition:** large editorial numerals separated by hairlines across a cream field.
- **Scroll / motion:** numbers count once on entry when motion is allowed; otherwise render instantly.
- **Desktop:** three figures share a baseline.
- **Mobile:** vertical sequence with generous spacing and smaller labels.
- **RTL:** numeral/label groups align right while Latin numerals remain legible.

## 5. Journal preview — “From the studio”

- **Purpose:** add freshness and reveal the thinking behind the work.
- **Composition:** one leading visual story and two compact editorial entries; no generic card grid.
- **Scroll / motion:** image crop reveals and quiet link movement.
- **Desktop:** large feature on one side, text index on the other.
- **Mobile:** feature image followed by a clean story list.
- **RTL:** metadata order and arrow direction adapt; dates remain locale-specific.

## 6. Closing statement and footer

- **Purpose:** leave a memorable brand promise and a clear contact path.
- **Composition:** peach-to-forest transition; oversized closing line, circular contact action, then a spacious footer with navigation, social, and contact details.
- **Scroll / motion:** closing words reveal line by line; footer rises naturally with no theatrical exit.
- **Desktop:** statement and action counterbalance horizontally.
- **Mobile:** action follows the statement and remains thumb-friendly.
- **RTL:** footer columns reorder semantically; legal copy and social alignment follow Arabic reading order.

## Navigation layer

The fullscreen menu uses forest as an immersive canvas, an architectural preview occupying one side, and numbered oversized links on the other. It opens with a curtain motion and staggered links, traps focus, closes with Escape, locks background scroll, and exposes language/contact actions. On mobile the image becomes a shallow atmospheric header and the links take priority.
